Poster design, ASSON

An official poster for Hon. Davis Emmanuel Edet, Chief Councilor and Head of the Legislative Council of ASSON, the Anatomical Students’ Association, University of Port Harcourt chapter.

The portrait sits on a light backdrop with the national flag and both crests. A maroon and gold banner carries his name and office, so the title can be read at a glance.
